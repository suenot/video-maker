# Source queue verification

The source worker reuses Gaia's saved Camoufox and existing NotebookLM helpers.
The final video renderer remains unchanged. No additional orchestration or
Pydantic dependency was needed for the observed failures; validation remains
at frozen-input, identity, submission and download boundaries.

Observed faults and corrections:

- Saved fingerprint/native bounds restored a small window. Read the usable
  display, back up native bounds, apply launch geometry and let the actual
  viewport follow the window. Measured live outer size: 1979x1290; inner:
  1939x1200; DPR: 2.
- NotebookLM shortened source labels and replaced temporary upload IDs.
  Reconcile the sole permanent ID against a saved insertion baseline and
  select it exactly. Preserve the intent during processing and do not upload
  again, including when temporary and permanent rows overlap.
- A delayed customize dialog exposed stale controls. Wait for its expected
  visible prompt, then verify the selected language, source and audio options.
  The actual language list can display lower-case "русский"; selection is
  exact and case-insensitive, followed by a retained-value check.
- A quota-limited customize dialog offers only Generate later. Verify that
  enabled control and quota text before saving the submission intent. Schedule
  once, preserve reset text/browser zone and bind its new artifact ID. Do not
  click the direct scheduled request again.
- The subsequent hard quota disables source insertion as well. Save its exact
  reset/browser zone, stop new notebooks/uploads until that time and continue
  reconciliation/downloads for already bound jobs. Bound source-helper calls
  to 120 seconds. The interrupted empty TWAP notebook was inspected and kept
  for reuse; its recovery evidence remains in state.
- Interrupted submissions could be mistaken for new jobs. Save mutation
  intent/baselines before the click and bind a new artifact only in the live
  observed submission pass. Uncertain unbound requests fail closed.
- A completed article could later block the whole queue after being edited or
  gaining a video. Verify completed draft file hashes and skip its generation
  checks; keep its original frozen input evidence.
- Repeated heartbeat polls opened Camoufox despite a saved hard-quota reset.
  Version 1.1.1 returns before loading browser helpers or profile settings
  until that time; only local original hashes are checked. This supersedes
  the earlier quota-period browser reconciliation behavior described above.
- A failed download could appear complete before inspection. Validate pending
  bytes with pdfinfo or ffprobe/full FFmpeg decode before atomic promotion.

Verification included eight exact full-article snapshots and 24 frozen input
hashes, syntax/JSON checks, 11 artifact-card/recovery cases, preserved unrelated
profile settings, an in-memory fingerprint copy, live-profile ownership
rejection, and completed-job continuation with generation checks deliberately
made to fail. The latter check opened no browser. The actual live run downloaded
bound PDF/audio originals and bound direct quota-scheduled artifacts. A prior
duplicate-worker check confirmed that the lock does not open another browser.
The final bounded pass exited with status 0: four completed PDF/audio pairs
were retained, four order-flow requests remained bound and scheduled, and the
missing TWAP source stage entered a recorded quota wait. The quota date check
also covers an evening observation with a reset after midnight.

Independent read-only review identified the continuation and quota edge cases
corrected above. Runtime logs and
